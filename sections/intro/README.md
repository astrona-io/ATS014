# Introduction

Before you start the first module, take ten minutes for this introduction. It tells you what this course will train you to do, how to get your machine ready, and who builds the course. You will not touch Istio here, but these three short pages make every page after them easier to follow.

The introduction has three pages, and they are best read in the order they come. **Welcome To The Course** explains who the course is for, what you can do at the end, the words the course uses, the example application, how the course is laid out, and how to read a page. **Get Your Machine Ready** lists the tools you need, the four `astrona` commands you will use every day, and where to get help. **How This Course Is Made** explains how the content is written and checked, who maintains it, and how to report a mistake.

When you are done, the course starts with the mesh foundations: how a request moves through the mesh.
