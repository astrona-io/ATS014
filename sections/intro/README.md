# Mission Briefing

Welcome aboard, astronaut. Before your first mission, take ten minutes for this briefing. It tells you what this course will train you to do, how to get your machine ready, and who builds the course.

You will not touch Istio here. These three short pages make every page after them easier to follow.

## The briefing

1. **[Welcome, Astronaut](./course-01-welcome.md)**: who this course is for, what you can do at the end, how the course is laid out, and how to read a page.
2. **[Before Your First Launch](./course-02-before-your-first-launch.md)**: the tools you need, the four `astrona` commands you will use every day, and where to get help.
3. **[How This Course Is Made](./course-03-how-this-course-is-made.md)**: how the content is written and checked, who maintains it, and how to report a mistake.

When you are done, start with [section 000, Mesh Foundations](../section-000/README.md).
